# The open answers: three edits, and one rule

Confirmed 2026-08-25: the six open questions were put to each participant in a closing interview,
and you entered a written record of each answer into the form.

That closes the integrity question. It opens a smaller and easier one: **Methodology currently
describes the open questions and the interview as two separate things, and they are one.**

---

## What the .docx says now

Section 3.5, the session sequence:

> "The comparison items and the open questions came after both conditions were finished, and a short
> interview closed the session."

Section 3.5, the instrument:

> "The questionnaire was delivered as a Google Form of six pages, and the pages exist because they
> are filled at different moments in the session rather than in one sitting. The experimenter tells
> the participant when to move on."

Appendix VI, the consent form, already says the participant takes part in *"a brief post-task
interview about how the two conditions compared."*

**So the interview is disclosed and consented to.** What is wrong is only that the open questions
are presented as a page the participant filled in, and as something distinct from the interview.

---

## Edit 1, the session sequence

**Replace:**

> The comparison items and the open questions came after both conditions were finished, and a short
> interview closed the session.

**With:**

> The comparison items came after both conditions were finished. The session closed with a short
> interview built on the six open questions, and the experimenter recorded each answer in writing.

## Edit 2, the instrument

**Append to the paragraph describing the Google Form:**

> The participant answered the background, questionnaire and comparison pages directly. The final
> page holds the open questions, and its responses were entered by the experimenter during the
> closing interview.

## Edit 3, one clause in 4.3

Wherever the open responses are first mentioned:

> The responses to the open questions are the experimenter's written record of the closing
> interview.

---

## 🔴 The rule that follows, and it is not optional

**No quotation marks, anywhere, for any of that text.** Not in the body, not in the appendix, not in
the Discussion. Every string in that column is your phrasing, including the long specific ones from
P02, P03 and P04 that read as individual. They are your record of what those participants said, not
what they said.

Two ways to use them, both legitimate:

1. **Report themes with counts.** "Six participants said the material information was of limited
   help on a prototype this simple." That is a fair summary of a record you made.
2. **Attribute the record explicitly.** "P02 described the application as disproportionate to a task
   this simple, recording that it would matter for a device where a mistake causes injury." No
   quotation marks, and the sentence says whose words it is.

⚠ If you still have the original interview notes with the participants' own words, a verbatim quote
becomes possible again, sourced to those notes rather than to the form. If you do not, option 1 or 2.
