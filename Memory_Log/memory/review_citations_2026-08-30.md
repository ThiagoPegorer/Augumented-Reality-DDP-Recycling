### Substantive point behind P1

The thesis states the data carrier must **sit physically on the product**. Art. 10(1)(b) says it shall be physically present **on the product, its packaging or on documentation accompanying the product**. That matters more than the pinpoint: the claim that the carrier is on the product is the premise of the AR anchoring argument. Either narrow the sentence to what the law says and note that a product-mounted carrier is the case your prototype assumes, or state that the delegated act for the product group will decide. As written an examiner can read it as the law guaranteeing something it does not.

## Verified correct, no action needed

Checked pinpoint by pinpoint against the OJ text. Do not spend time re-checking these.

**Directive 2012/19/EU:** Art. 7 and recital 17 (collection rate, 65 % of three-year average or 85 % of WEEE generated). Art. 2(4), point (d) (means of transport for persons or goods, excluding non-type-approved electric two-wheel vehicles). *I expected this one to be point (g) and it is not. Your citation is right.*

**Directive 2000/53/EC:** Art. 3(1) scope. Art. 7(2)(b) 95 % and 85 % from 1 January 2015. Art. 8(1) and (2) coding standards. Art. 8(3) dismantling information within six months, components, materials and location of hazardous substances. Art. 8(4) component manufacturers, `as far as it is requested by these facilities`. Art. 2(13) definition of dismantling information, carrying the `manuals or ... electronic media (e.g. CD-ROM, on-line services)` wording. Annex I removal list.

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1781:** Art. 7 *Information requirements*. Art. 75(2), evaluation by 19 July 2030 expressly covering the vehicles referred to in Art. 1(2), point (h).

**Regulation (EU) 2026/1738:** Art. 11(1) and Art. 11(3). Art. 13 and Art. 13(6), implementing acts by 14 August 2030, `in a manner that does not preclude any technological solution`. Art. 29(1). Art. 29(2). Art. 33, including the exclusion of batteries and the 30 % plastics target from 1 January 2032. Annex VI, points 4 and 5. Annex VIII, Part C, points 12 and 19(b), and the exemption marking on 19(b).
