# 🔴 TWO NEW VOICE RULES, learned from his edits this session

### 12. Translating numbers into words is not translating numbers into meaning
His instruction: *"the numbers was already mentioned extensively in the results chapter, here lets
translate the numbers in sentences and what does it mean."* The first attempt replaced digits with
verbal quantities ("less than half of one percent"), which is still Results, just spelled out. **He
rejected it as not explicit.** What a Discussion paragraph needs is a flat interpretive sentence
stating a conclusion the reader could disagree with, then the evidence. Not a verbal restatement.

### 13. A Discussion paragraph opens on HIS result; the source arrives inside the sentence
His verdict on the first full 5.1: *"It almost sound a literature review, then a proper discussion.
In discussion I want just to use the literature review to sustain the results."* The defect was
eight sources with several paragraphs opening on the author. **Never put a cited author in the
subject slot of a Discussion paragraph's first sentence.** Cut any source that only decorates.

⚠ Also confirmed this session: **he will not accept block-by-block drafting for a whole section.** His
words: *"doing the paragraphs per block is a risk in a way that we will not tell a history in a logic
way."* Propose the spine, let him rewrite it, then draft the whole section in one pass.
