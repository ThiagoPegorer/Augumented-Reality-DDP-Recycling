The results reported in this section come from computing the life cycle model described in the

## PARAGRAPH 2, unchanged from [380]

Characterization under EF 3.1 produced a result for each of the sixteen midpoint impact categories
(Andreasi Bassi et al., 2023, p. 5). The method also supplies normalization factors, which express a
result as a share of the impacts of a reference unit, and weighting factors, which reflect the
perceived relative importance of each category (Andreasi Bassi et al., 2023, p. 5). Together these
make the sixteen categories comparable with one another on a single scale. The study applied the
most relevant impact categories procedure of the Environmental Footprint method to that ranking
(European Commission, 2021, Annex I, 6.3.1). Categories are ordered by their normalized and weighted
contribution, and the smallest set that cumulatively reaches eighty percent of the total is treated
as the most relevant. This study reports that set together with the category that crosses the
threshold.
