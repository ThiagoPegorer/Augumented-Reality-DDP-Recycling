## TEXT 1 — after the stage 1 to 4 table and the pie figure

Within stages 1 to 4 the distribution differs by category. In resource use, minerals and
metals, stage 1 accounts for 97.8433 % of the four stage subtotal, stage 4 for 1.7114 %,
stage 2 for 0.4450 % and stage 3 for 0.0003 %. In climate change the subtotal divides
between stage 1 at 52.5384 % and stage 4 at 46.8216 %, with stage 2 at 0.6018 % and stage 3
at 0.0381 %. In freshwater eutrophication stage 1 accounts for 53.9435 %, stage 4 for
45.5643 %, stage 2 for 0.4906 % and stage 3 for 0.0016 %.

Stages 2 and 3 fall below the angular width the chart can render in all three categories,
so no wedge is visible for them. Their values are printed beneath each chart.

The shares above are taken on the stage 1 to 4 subtotal. Measured against the Sc1 gross
result, that subtotal is 99.9998 % in resource use, minerals and metals, 99.7596 % in
climate change and 99.9981 % in freshwater eutrophication. The remainder in each category
is stage 5.

## TEXT 2 — after the stage 5 share table, introducing the three process tables

Stage 5 holds a different set of processes in each scenario. Sc1 holds two, the sanitary
landfill of inert waste and the waste plastic market. Sc2 holds four, adding WEEE shredding
and the electronics scrap copper smelter. Sc3 and Sc4 hold five, replacing WEEE shredding
with a share of a manual treatment facility and with dismantling electricity. The tables
below list every process and the share it holds of that scenario's own stage 5 result, one
table per reported category, with the absolute stage 5 total on the final row. A process
that does not occur in a scenario is marked n/a.

In resource use, minerals and metals, WEEE shredding holds 86.6857 % of the Sc2 stage 5
result, and the electronics scrap copper smelter holds 53.2725 % under Sc3 and 45.8960 %
under Sc4. In climate change the waste plastic market holds the largest share in all four
scenarios, at 96.5497 %, 76.8264 %, 49.8479 % and 49.5290 %. In freshwater eutrophication
the largest share is the sanitary landfill under Sc1 at 82.4436 %, WEEE shredding under Sc2
at 91.5753 %, and dismantling electricity under Sc3 and Sc4 at 66.2459 % and 80.3213 %.

The dismantling electricity input is an assumed value of 0.01 kWh under Sc3 and 0.02 kWh
under Sc4. The shares in these tables are taken on each scenario's own stage 5 result and
are not comparable with the shares in the preceding table, which are taken on the gross
result.
