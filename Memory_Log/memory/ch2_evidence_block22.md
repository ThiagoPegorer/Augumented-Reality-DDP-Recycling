# THE WEEE DIRECTIVE EXCLUDES VEHICLES. Verified 2026-08-14.

`CELEX_32012L0019_EN_TXT.pdf` **p. 6, Article 2(4)(d)** `[P]`: the Directive "shall not apply to…
**means of transport for persons or goods**, excluding electric two-wheel vehicles which are not
type-approved". Full regulatory picture in eu_regulatory_scope.

Consequences: automotive electronics are **not counted** in the WEEE figures; the correct transition
in prose is a **discontinuity**, not a continuation; and Chapter 1 moves from WEEE figures into
automotive electrification without marking the scope change, which needs one sentence added.

# `[P]` Restrepo et al. (2019), `resources-08-00058.pdf` — deep read

**Swiss dataset**: 65,475 car models sold in Switzerland over 14 years; funded by the Swiss Federal
Office for the Environment. State the geography. Switzerland is **not** the EU and may not carry a
claim about EU law; present it as a parallel jurisdiction (the SRH contract asks for parallels).

**Penetration (p. 17):** regulation-affected systems (stop-start, ESP) rose rapidly to high levels;
market-left systems (navigation, cruise control) held at intermediate levels for years;
automated-driving systems grew fast but were **under 5 % of new cars in 2015**. Four best predictors:
**price, mass, engine displacement (or power), production year**; fuel type is not among them.
Because price and mass are market-segment proxies, "shaped by regulation rather than consumer
demand" **overstates it**. Defensible: *regulation determines which systems become universal; market
position determines which cars carry the rest.*
Abstract (p. 1): comfort-related below **40 %** after 14 years, safety-mandated approaching **100 %**,
emission-related about **60 % after six years**.

**Integration, the major finding:**
- **p. 2:** a new system may be "dedicated electronic control units (ECUs), **integrated circuits in
  existing ECUs, or as mere software add-ons**"; this challenges "the accounting of the number of
  embedded ECS as well as of the mass and composition of their related EE devices".
- **p. 10, ABS → ESP:** standalone ABS fell from **almost 60 % in 2001 to under 20 % in 2015**; the
  **ESP reached about 80 % by 2015**; the two **combined near 100 % since 2005**. Implied standalone
  ABS "lifetime" about **40 years**.
- **p. 16, MASS DECOUPLED FROM VALUE:** the number of physical ECUs is likely to **decrease** and
  devices to get **lighter**, "the total stocks and flows of CRMs in cars may nevertheless increase"
  because penetration rises across all car types. Supports the burden-weighted frame.
- **p. 16, their policy conclusion:** a mandatory dismantling list "would need to be updated on a
  regular basis" and would work better "at the level of **car functions** instead of… specific EE
  devices".
- **p. 2, THE THESIS PREMISE STATED BY A THIRD PARTY IN 2019:** "The use of ECS and corresponding EE
  devices in vehicles, as well as their composition, is **poorly understood, which hampers the
  development of recycling strategies for CRMs in cars**."

# `[P]` Oke & Potgieter (2024), `Discarded e-waste printed circuit boards.pdf`

p. 4: Au, Ag and Pd are "about **90.0 % of their inherent value**" in high/medium-grade discarded
PCBs; Ta capacitors are "about **30.0-40.0 wt%**" tantalum and about **34 % of world Ta production**
went into them in 2016. **p. 6, THE COUNTER-EVIDENCE:** manual dismantling shows "low disassembly
efficiency", risks "electrolytic capacitor explosions and the release of poisonous gases", and
"fails to meet the prerequisites of safe production and presents difficulties in achieving
large-scale application". ⚠ That last string is VERBATIM; quote it or rewrite it.

# `[P]` Peitzmeier et al. (2025) · Chancerel et al. (2009) · Harkema et al. (2024)

Peitzmeier p. 1 "computers on wheels", Physical Component Mapping + Eco-Sensitivity Framework, and
20-30 % of e-waste collected (a range, never present as confirming GEM's 22.3 %). p. 2: Esteva et al.
(2020)'s framework "lacks specific details regarding vehicle electronics, both at the product and
component level". p. 6 spent in Chapter 1 (glued joints).

Chancerel p. 2: "Preprocessing determines which fractions and thus substances of WEEE are steered
into appropriate end-processing streams. Any sorting of a specific target substance into the 'wrong'
output stream from preprocessing in most cases leads to the loss of this substance in the final
recovery step."

Harkema, S., Rensing, P. A., Domensino, S. M. D. C., Vermeijlen, J. M., Godoi Bizarro, D. E., &
van Schaik, A. (2024). *Journal of Cleaner Production*, 450, 141837. **p. 2:** encapsulation in
plastic protects against corrosion and damage "but creates a significant challenge at End-of-Life
(EoL) to recycle such in-plastic embedded electronics". **p. 1:** making the encapsulant removable
"goes against the requirement of high adhesion over the entire surface area for structural strength
and integrity". Their fix is a non-adhering disassembly layer tuned through vias, a compromise not an
escape. Also p. 2: LCAs showing 34 % carbon reduction for an in-moulded automotive control panel and
20-31 % for a coffee machine panel (secondary citations, candidates for the LCA method block).

# The opinion camp and the reconciliation

1. **Manual dismantling does not scale and is unsafe** — Oke (2024) p. 6.
2. **Going finer buys almost nothing** — Alcoceba-Pascual (2025) p. 8: shredding 0.03 % / 5 %, car
   dismantling 59 % / 49 %, part disassembly 59 % / 51 %.
3. **Automate the selection** — Maurice, A. A., Dinh, K. N., Charpentier, N. M., Brambilla, A., &
   Gabriel, J.-C. P. (2021), *Sustainability* 13, 10357, **p. 18**: Ramon et al.'s machine-vision +
   CNN + robotic scraper for Ta capacitors, EUR 60,000 initial investment.

**Reconciliation. Charles, R. G., Douglas, P., Dowling, M., Liversage, G., & Davies, M. L. (2020)**,
*Resources, Conservation & Recycling* 161, 104923, **p. 16** `[P]`: manual isolation of all SMDs is
"time intensive and prohibitively expensive" but "may be suitable for 'cherry picking' of individual
components identified as containing CRMs not targeted in PM recovery (e.g. Ta capacitors)". The
literature rejects *exhaustive* manual disassembly, not *selective* removal. ⚠ Both strings are
VERBATIM; quote or rewrite.
